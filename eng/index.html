ℹ️ About
Mawlidi 🌙

Mawlidi is an independent Arabic digital project offering precise tools to calculate Hijri age, alongside cultural and educational content that helps people understand the human connection to time.

We believe that a person is far more complex than their birth date, and that character is shaped by faith, upbringing, environment, and life experiences.

This site makes no predictions or claims of knowing the unseen — only God knows that.

✦ Our Values
